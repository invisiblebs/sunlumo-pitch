Methodology

Four steps from
data to deployment

01
Behavioural Prior Extraction

Ingest your CRM, email history, support logs, and webshop data. Extract empirical base rates per installer archetype: reply rates, objection patterns, channel preferences, deal cycles. These are hard constraints, not generated by the model.

02
Constrained Persona Construction

Each archetype combines a factual demographic shell (from trade registers and enrichment sources), an empirical behavioural cage (from your data), and an LLM-generated conversational layer that operates only inside those constraints. The model decides how a persona phrases an objection — it does not decide whether they object.

03
Simulation as Aggregation

Multi-step sequences run against the persona cohort. Outcomes are counted, not invented. A predicted reply rate is the descriptive average across simulated runs, with a confidence interval — never a number the LLM has guessed.

04
Retrodiction and Live Updating

Before any forward prediction is trusted, the system reconstructs your historical campaigns and is measured against actual outcomes. After deployment, every real result tightens priors via Bayesian updating, with calibration tracked on an auditable dashboard.

Campaign Sequencing

Warmest signals first

The pilot starts with the most legally and commercially safe pocket. Most competing pitches invert this and put your brand at risk in Q1.

1
Existing-customer reactivation
Triggered by intent signals — webshop behaviour and deanonymisation. Most compliant, highest conversion probability, zero brand risk.
2
Trade-show & subsidy-event follow-up
Warm contacts who have opted into communication. Subsidy calendar awareness creates natural urgency without manufactured pressure.
3
Cold outbound to net-new prospects
Sequenced last, and only after warm-pipeline economics are proven. Consent infrastructure in place. Retrodiction accepted. Brand safe.
