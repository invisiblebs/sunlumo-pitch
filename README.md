# Sunlumo AI Sales Challenge: Proposal

## The opportunity

The defining failure mode of autonomous B2B sales in 2025 is well-documented: generic AI copy, hallucinated personalisation, burned deliverability, and damaged brand reputation. The structural cause is the same across every major platform; campaigns deploy first and learn later. For US SaaS teams optimising for volume that is tolerable. For a European battery distributor selling into a fragmented installer market where partner trust matters more than reply count, it is the wrong architecture entirely.

Our proposal is built on a different principle. **Before any message reaches a real installer, it is tested against synthetic installer personas grounded in your own historical data.** No major sales platform on the market today (Artisan, 11x, Clay, Apollo, Outreach, Salesloft, Cognism, Amplemarket, Lemlist, Humanlinker) ships pre-deployment campaign simulation. That is the gap we build into.

## What we are building

A simulation-first agentic sales operating system, purpose-built for the European distributor-to-installer motion. It runs as a small set of specialised AI agents (research, simulation, outreach, feedback) under a central supervisor, exposed through a visual workflow canvas where your team designs and approves campaigns without writing code. Every campaign passes through a mandatory simulation step that forecasts reply rates, channel performance, and conversion before deployment. Real outcomes flow back through Bayesian updating to tighten the model, campaign by campaign.

Compliance presets (UWG, GDPR), native multilingual generation, a solar-vertical knowledge layer covering product compatibility, subsidy programmes, and certification requirements, plus channel-aware routing across email, WhatsApp Business, and human-in-loop LinkedIn are built in. US-built generalists are structurally disqualified by language, compliance, and vertical depth; European generalists lack the vertical layer.

## Methodology in four steps

1. **Behavioural prior extraction.** Ingest your CRM, email history, support logs, and webshop data. Extract empirical base rates per installer archetype: reply rates, objection patterns, channel preferences, deal cycles. These are hard constraints, not generated by the model.
2. **Constrained persona construction.** Each archetype combines a factual demographic shell (from trade registers and enrichment sources), an empirical behavioural cage (from your data), and an LLM-generated conversational layer that operates only inside those constraints. The model decides how a persona phrases an objection; it does not decide whether they object.
3. **Simulation as aggregation.** Multi-step sequences are run against the persona cohort, and outcomes are counted, not invented. A predicted reply rate is the descriptive average across simulated runs, with a confidence interval, never a number the LLM has guessed.
4. **Retrodiction and live updating.** Before any forward prediction is trusted, the system reconstructs your historical campaigns and is measured against actual outcomes. After deployment, every real result tightens priors via Bayesian updating, with calibration tracked on a dashboard you can audit.

## Sequencing: warmest signals first

The pilot starts deliberately with the most legally and commercially safe pocket: existing-customer reactivation triggered by intent signals (webshop, deanonymisation). Trade-show follow-up and subsidy-event campaigns come next; cold outbound to net-new prospects is sequenced last and only after warm-pipeline economics are proven. This ordering is a design choice; most competing pitches will invert it and put your brand at risk in the first quarter.

## What we will not do

- No mass cold outbound in regulated markets before consent infrastructure is in place.
- No LinkedIn automation that violates platform terms; we use Sales Navigator manually for high-value accounts.
- No simulation predictions claimed as accurate before historical retrodiction has passed our internal acceptance threshold.
- No "AI replaces your team" framing; the system augments your KAM team and escalates ambiguous replies with full context.

## Commercial structure

The contract is phased and milestone-gated, sized inside the range named in the brief.

- **Phase 0, build and calibrate (months 1 to 3):** fixed scope. Covers infrastructure, prior extraction, simulation engine, canvas, first templates, retrodiction validation, and a controlled A/B against your current best-practice sequence.
- **Phase 1, DACH scale (months 4 to 6):** regional rollout, trade-show and subsidy-trigger workflows, public-facing knowledge-graph asset.
- **Phase 2, expansion (months 7 to 12):** additional European markets, each onboarded as a separate calibration run with its own KPI commitment.
- **Phase 3, long-term partnership (month 13 onward):** renewable annual engagement covering model maintenance, new channels, and continued expansion.

Performance is tracked against qualified partner registrations and resulting orders, measured against your current cost baseline. The simulation layer is designed to outperform untested cold outreach, but the magnitude is something we measure together rather than promise upfront. Phase gates between each stage allow either side to review the engagement openly if a phase misses its KPI.
